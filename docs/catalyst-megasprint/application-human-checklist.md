# Human checklist before submitting the Catalyst application

The linked application file is a review copy. The original draft was left unchanged; no application was submitted.

## Confirm applicant-only facts

- Confirm the applicant name, preferred title, contact details, affiliation and eligibility statements in the form.
- Confirm the computational-biology experience, the personal account that your own research and software work led you to this problem, and whether you can add a verified example or estimate of interruption frequency/time lost. Also confirm thesis DOCX use and sole-founder/no-cofounder/no-adviser description. These are personal claims, not independently verifiable product evidence.
- Confirm that “built by Adam” accurately describes material product contributions; disclose any contributor the form asks about.
- Confirm the statements that there are no paid customers or external institutional validation.
- Confirm the initial-buyer hypothesis of independent computational researchers and small research teams, including whether the individual researcher or a team lead is the proposed payer. Confirm Codex Desktop is the current MCP host you want to name and that the described local tools are in use; change the host wording if needed.
- Decide whether pricing remains unvalidated as written or whether to include the earlier roughly $20/month hosted-plan hypothesis. If used, label it a hypothesis and confirm what the hosted and local/self-hosted plans include.
- Confirm that £25,000 and the four budget allocations are still the intended ask. Before recruitment, name the single workflow/tools covered by the £12,000 integration and what is excluded; confirm the cost basis. Define cohort size, recruitment source, inclusion criteria, duration, matched task, order/counterbalancing, and time start/stop. Select the primary outcome and either a feasible success threshold or explicitly frame the study as feasibility-only. Set measure numerators/denominators and capture methods (including logging usual-workflow actions, repeated-action definitions, planned-versus-observed discrepancies, seeded-gap misses/false alarms and reviewer burden).
- Define participant consent and what plan, approval and action-trace data will be collected, de-identified, retained and deleted. Specify the independent reviewer’s independence, access, security-review scope and written deliverable. Confirm eligible uses and a rough cost cap for the £3,000 infrastructure allocation.
- Confirm the proposed pilot can capture selected plans, step outcomes and human approvals, and measure planned-versus-observed discrepancies as described.

## Review the demo and product claims

- Review the [demo rehearsal](demo-rehearsal.md). Confirm permission to show the DNABERT/Paper 1 materials, the PLOS figure include, and the description as a read-only trace. Do not present it as a Meridian-linked workflow or as scientific validation.
- Resolve the manuscript venue/canonical branch before any live demonstration. The rehearsal reports an internal, AI-assisted scaffold that is not author-approved.
- Re-read the [claim-evidence ledger](claim-evidence-ledger.md) and [handoff lifecycle contract](handoff-lifecycle-contract.md). Preserve their distinctions between shipped components, incomplete joins, unknown runtime exposure and proposed pilot behavior.
- Ensure the application does not imply that Meridian provisions research compute, automatically recommends experiments, has a fully joined project/artifact graph, provides production backup, or has verified live Overleaf or end-to-end Zotero/PDF integration.

## Form and submission check

- Open the [official Catalyst call and application link](https://www.digital-science.com/about-us/investment/catalyst-grant/). The published closing time is Monday 5 October 2026 at 17:00 BST (16:00 UTC / 11:00 CDT).
- Confirm the live form remains open before entering responses.
- Paste only the nine proposal sections from [application-review-copy.md](application-review-copy.md), not this checklist or the review-copy preamble. Keep the official headings: The Problem; Your Workflow; Trust, Audit and Governance; Team; Where You Are Today; Alternatives and Competitors; Where This Goes; Fit with Digital Science; Budget.
- The current proposal text is below 1,500 words by whitespace count; check the live form’s own counter after making edits and leave margin.
- Verify all numeric totals, links, contact fields, declarations, eligibility answers and any requested demo attachment. Save a copy of the exact submitted text and confirmation receipt.
- Adam, as applicant, makes the final accuracy decision and submits the form.
