Off (the default): Attach PDFs from folder copies each PDF into Zotero storage. On: the PDF stays where it is and Zotero links to it. Duplicate policy for BibTeX import, applied before anything is written. An entry is skipped as an exact duplicate when its DOI matches an item already in the library, or when neither side has a DOI and the normalised title matches. An entry whose title matches an existing item, but where only one side has a DOI or both DOIs differ, is not imported; it is listed in the report for review. Everything else is imported.
